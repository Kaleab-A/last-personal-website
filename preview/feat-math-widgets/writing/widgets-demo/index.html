Fourier series

Any reasonable periodic function is a sum of sines. Each sine is a circle spinning at a whole-number speed; stack the circles tip to tail and the last tip traces the sum.

A tiny neural network

Two inputs, a handful of tanh⁡\tanhtanh units, one sigmoid output, trained with plain gradient descent on cross-entropy. The shading is the network’s confidence that a point belongs to the filled class.
