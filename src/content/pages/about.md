I'm Kaleab. I'm from Ethiopia, and I'm a senior at Vanderbilt University, double
majoring in computer science and mathematics.

I work where machine learning, mathematics and neuroscience meet. In Vanderbilt's
[Attention-Circuits-Control Lab](https://accl.psy.vanderbilt.edu/), where I've
been since 2023, I decode behavior from primate neural recordings. I also use
topological data analysis to study brain imaging and language-model features.
Over two summers as an ML Foundations intern at Dropbox, I built infrastructure
for serving and evaluating language models. I also TA algorithms courses.

Long term, I want to do a PhD in AI/ML theory, on the mathematical foundations of
machine learning. Along the way, when I learn something the hard way, I try to
write it up so the next person doesn't have to.

The [research](/research) page has the details, and the [CV](/cv) has the short
version.
