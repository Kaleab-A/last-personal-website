Posts can include small interactive figures. Each one is a React component
that loads only when you scroll to it. Import it at the top of the `.mdx` file
and use it with `client:visible`.

## Gradient descent

Gradient descent repeats one update, $x_{t+1} = x_t - \eta \nabla f(x_t)$. On a
quadratic bowl $f(x) = \tfrac12 x^\top A x$ the whole story is in the
eigenvalues of $A$. This bowl has $\lambda = 1$ and $\lambda = 8$, so anything
past $\eta = 2/8$ blows up, and anything past $1/8$ overshoots the steep
direction on every step.

## Fourier series

Any reasonable periodic function is a sum of sines. Each sine is a circle
spinning at a whole-number speed; stack the circles tip to tail and the last tip
traces the sum.

## A tiny neural network

Two inputs, a handful of $\tanh$ units, one sigmoid output, trained with plain
gradient descent on cross-entropy. The shading is the network's confidence that
a point belongs to the filled class.
