Sign language motion generation (Kaggle)

Generating sign-language motion as discrete RVQ-VAE tokens. I moved from a custom autoregressive transformer to MaskGIT with classifier-free guidance; best leaderboard score about 0.416 R-Precision.

  • Bidirectional attention beat autoregressive decoding on this task.
  • Classifier-free guidance has to be built in during training.
  • Retrieval baselines were surprisingly hard to beat.
