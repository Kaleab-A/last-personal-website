Research

Decoding trial outcomes from macaque neural activity

paper in progress

Predicting whether a trial was correct or an error from multiunit activity in the anterior cingulate cortex, prefrontal cortex and caudate, recorded with silicon probes while monkeys did a feature-learning task.

  • Main model: a custom logistic regression with an L2 penalty that keeps the weights smooth across time and an L1 penalty that keeps them sparse, fit with proximal gradient descent. The weights stay stable over the trial, and each channel’s contribution is easy to interpret.
  • Baselines: QDA on PCA features, distance-weighted k-NN and Gaussian Naive Bayes.
  • Validation: nested cross-validation with StratifiedGroupKFold to avoid leakage, scored with balanced accuracy.

Methods and tools: Neural decoding · Logistic regression · Proximal gradient descent · Sparse models · Nested cross-validation · Python · scikit-learn

Neural signatures of feature learning

in progress

How monkeys learn which visual feature (color, pattern or shape) is rewarded, and how they move from exploring to exploiting.

  • Trials are aligned to a “learning point” instead of by trial number: the first trial where the monkey reaches 80% accuracy over the next 10 trials.
  • Data: 40 probes in the anterior cingulate cortex, 50 channels each. Each probe gets its own PCA (5–10 components) and its own logistic regression, and the results are combined across probes.
  • Also looks at credit assignment: how the previous trial’s outcome shapes processing on the current one.

Methods and tools: Computational neuroscience · PCA · Logistic regression · Python · MATLAB
